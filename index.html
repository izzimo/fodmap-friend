How to read your result

Likely suitable No obvious high-FODMAP ingredient detected. Not a guarantee.

Check carefully An ingredient, portion size or incomplete label needs checking.

Potential concern One or more likely high-FODMAP ingredients were detected, or a strong one appears early in the list.

Insufficient data There isn't enough information for a useful assessment.

Monash University testing

This app does not have direct access to Monash's proprietary tested-food database. It will not claim a product is Monash-tested unless evidence is supplied. Ingredient screening is not laboratory testing.
